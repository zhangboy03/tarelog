# Governance

Tarelog is currently a maintainer-led project.

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** Boyu Zhang (`@zhangboy03`) sets release scope, reviews security reports, and merges changes.
- **Contributors:** anyone submitting issues, documentation, design, code, testing, or review.

## Decisions

Small reversible decisions happen in pull requests. Changes to data ownership, privacy, authentication, nutrition-source policy, licensing, or project direction begin in a public issue so trade-offs are recorded before implementation.

The maintainer has final merge authority while the project has one active maintainer. Decisions should cite user impact and verifiable evidence, not title or seniority.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no fixed contribution count. Sustained contributors who demonstrate careful review, respect privacy boundaries, and reliably maintain an area may be invited as maintainers. The role and scope will be recorded here.

## Continuity

If the current maintainer steps away, a successor will be chosen from active trusted contributors and announced in the repository. Until then, the MIT-licensed history remains forkable.
